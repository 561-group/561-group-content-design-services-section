# 561 Group Design Template Catalog

Templates are starting structures, not finished pieces. Fill their placeholders
before publication. A contributor may fork a template or write a new one; a new
template becomes a standing entry only after the council has approved a third
use. The full visual examples are in §08 of the
[v0.3 rendered design bible](561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s08).

- **Slogan poster** — 18 × 24 in, ink plus alarm; for wheatpaste, gallery,
  demonstration, or window use.
- **Assembly call** — 11 × 17 in, ink ground with reverse type and an alarm
  date; for meetings, hearings, gatherings, and assemblies.
- **Numeral banner** — 18 × 24 in poster or 36 × 48 in banner, ink only; for
  presence claims where the numeral carries the message.
- **Field-instruction pamphlet** — 8.5 × 11 in tri-fold, ink with alarm step
  numerals; for instruction-bearing artifacts.
- **Static web site** — static HTML, no framework or build step, twelve-column
  grid, under 200 KB, and functional without JavaScript.
