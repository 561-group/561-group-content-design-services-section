# Stamp variant — alarm, single-color

The stamp variant of the [561 mark](five-six-one-mark.md) is a single-color, framed lockup struck in [alarm-red](alarm-red.md) on physical objects 561 produces or processes.

## When required

Physical objects 561 produces or processes: shipped crates, supply boxes, distributed pamphlet covers, hand-stamped envelopes, doors of held space.
The stamp asserts physical authorship.

## When permitted

Only on physical objects, only as a single-color print or hand-stamp impression, only in [alarm-red](alarm-red.md).
Forbidden on digital surfaces.
Forbidden on certificates of any kind, including ones we make ourselves.
Forbidden as a "verified by 561" mark — 561 does not certify.

## Geometry

Square, side length *S*.

- Outer frame: `--ems-rule-beam` (3 px / 0.75 mm) inset `--ems-u1` (4 px / 1 mm) from trim.
- Inner frame: `--ems-rule-hair` inset `--ems-u2` (8 px / 2 mm) from outer frame.
- Numeral cap-height: 0.42 *S*.
- Numeral baseline at vertical center of the inner frame.
- Separator rule: `--ems-rule-hair`, full inner-frame width, 0.10 *S* below numeral baseline.
- Wordmark "GROUP": cap-height 0.07 *S*, tracking +0.30 em, baseline 0.10 *S* below separator rule.

## Color

Alarm-red only.
Single-color impression.
Misregistration is a property of the medium and is not a defect at this scale.

If alarm ink is unavailable (e.g., field conditions), defer the stamp; do not substitute black.

## Check

Hand-stamp on uncoated kraft at *S* = 50 mm.
Mark must remain legible after five sequential re-inkings without re-charging the die.
(See also [photocopy-survival theorem](../561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09) for the analogous test on the primary mark.)
