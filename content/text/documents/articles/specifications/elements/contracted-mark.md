# Contracted mark — sub-minimum scale

The contracted variant of the [561 mark](five-six-one-mark.md), used when the artifact's available mark area is below the minimum (cap-height *H* < 32 px screen / 8 mm print) and scaling up the artifact is not possible — favicons, app icons, embroidered tags, stamped hardware.

## Form

Numeral only, no bracket rule, no [GROUP wordmark](group-wordmark.md).
Family unchanged ([Space Grotesk 700](type-stack.md)).
Tracking opens to −0.02 em (less tight than primary, to keep glyphs distinct at small size).

- **Favicon:** 32 × 32 px, numeral cap-height 22 px, optically centered.
- **App icon:** 1024 × 1024 px source, numeral cap-height 580 px, optically centered, on [bone](bone.md) ground with no border.

## Forbidden at contracted scale

- Any rendering of the bracket rule.
- Any rendering of "GROUP".
- Any background other than [ink](ink.md) or [bone](bone.md).
- Any rounded-corner mask other than what the host OS imposes.
