# Witness blue

The bible's evidence color.
Token `--blue` (also called *witness*).
Hex `#3D5588`.
RGB 61 · 85 · 136.
CMYK 85 · 65 · 15 · 25.
Pantone 534 U.

Witness flags evidence.
It is *functional*, not decorative — it carries one job and is rationed.

## Use

- Figure callouts (`FIG 03.x`).
- Data quantities.
- Source links.
- Dates of record.
- Documentary subjects in [duotone treatment](../procedures/duotone-treatment.md) (ink + witness).

## Don't

- As a body link color.
- As a fill.
- Anywhere a sentence is not pointing at a fact.

## Coverage cap

≤ 3 % of inked area per page or per spread.
Same measurement method as [alarm-red](alarm-red.md): sum bounding boxes, divide by trim area.
Captions and callouts only.
See [color-budget](../../../../glossary/terms/color-budget.md) and the [color-budget-application morphism](../procedures/color-budget-application.md).

## Print notes

- **Riso:** Federal Blue is the closest stock to witness.
- **Photocopy:** Witness reproduces as a mid-gray on B&W copiers, which is acceptable — witness usage is not load-bearing for first-pass legibility (alarm carries that load).
- **Digital:** sRGB hex value directly.
  No P3, no glows, no gradients.

## Contrast

Witness on [bone](bone.md) is 7.6 : 1 — AAA.
Body-size callouts in witness are permitted.
Witness on [ink](ink.md) is not specified by the bible (witness on a dark ground is rare and should be reset to [alarm-red](alarm-red.md) or [bone](bone.md) on ink).

## Forbidden

- Witness as a fill (the duotone treatment uses witness as an *ink*, not as a flat ground).
- Witness on the [561 mark](five-six-one-mark.md) (the mark refuses witness color states).
- Witness in a [slogan](../../../../glossary/terms/slogan-as-argument.md) instance.
- Witness as a "calmer" alternative to alarm.
  Alarm and witness are not interchangeable.
