# § 09.1 — No philanthropic visual language

## Refusal

Soft-focus subjects looking grateful or in need.
White display type set over a tinted photograph.
Mission-statement copy paired with smiling-face composition.
The visual grammar of the donor appeal is forbidden.

## Why

The donor-appeal register reads as a request for sympathy from a presumed wealthy reader.
561's audience is not the donor and 561's voice is not the supplicant.
The register is also a stylistic attractor — without active refusal, the page drifts toward it every proof cycle (see [refusal-as-design-method](../../../../glossary/terms/refusal-as-design-method.md)).

## Check

Cover the artifact's text.
If what remains looks like a fundraising mailer, reset the photograph to evidentiary register (see [photograph-treatment](../elements/photograph-treatment.md) and [duotone-treatment](../procedures/duotone-treatment.md)) or remove imagery.
