# Open 561 Group Design Questions

These are known gaps. A question becomes a procedure or policy only when the
council has evidence of the failure it needs to prevent.

- What governs moving image and motion?
- What governs audio and radio production?
- When 561 holds physical space, what governs signage and wayfinding?
- How does the Latin-first type stack extend to non-Latin scripts?
- When may a secondary glyph appear without the primary mark?
- What discipline governs data visualization?
- What is the web dark-mode contract?
