# Derive, fabricate, and receive a branded artifact

This procedure answers a specific repeated production job: **a real customer or
enterprise wants a page, asset, book, report, storefront, or other artifact that
remains recognizable as the correct brand while meeting the reader's actual
needs.** It is a use-and-improve *design procedure inside the original 561 Group
content-design corpus*, not a second brand registry, source of graphics, Work
queue, renderer, or license to overwrite a consumer's own voice.

Select it through original Work and the existing Coesite
[improve-research-technology-practice operation](https://github.com/coesite-operations/operations-services-section/pull/352)
and [Meet-then-Beat method](https://github.com/coesite-operations/operations-services-section/blob/main/lib/agency/skills/meet-then-beat/SKILL.md).
The current original design source is this corpus's
[Design Bible v0.3](../561-group-design-bible.md) and
[DTCG 2025.10 tokens](../../../../../data/records/design-tokens/561-group.tokens.json).
The illustrated Drive **v0.4 candidate** provides research and design evidence,
not an automatically adopted new edition.

## One source-qualified commissioning tuple

Recover, rather than invent, these inputs from the original Work, actual
consumer and competent rights holder:

| Name | Required reading | Refusal case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receiving job | The person or agent and action that the finished artifact makes easier | "Make it look prestigious" without audience/job |
| Source and voice | Brand owner, active edition/revision, voice and forbidden claims | A visual theme treated as authority to make sociopolitical or commercial claims |
| Material | Format/viewport/medium, figure(s), photographs, permissions, provenance, source data | Borrowed images or paid assets without a verified right |
| External baseline | Current alternative serving **the same reader job**, exact version/context, positive and negative user evidence | A style adjective offered as proof of competitor parity |
| Conformance | Versioned standard/profile and bounded measurable acceptance tests | A generic "passes WCAG" badge with no actual audit |
| Receiving | Competent independent recipient, evidence format, actual version, correction path | The builder checking "approved" on their own source PR |

Different clients may select different brands. **The Group's v0.3
tokens must never be imposed on an external customer's requested identity.**
A consented client identity and its applicable rules are parameters; where
absent, offer a clearly labeled proposal rather than fabricate approval.

## Research gesture — derive requirements from the job, not vibes

1. Read the originating request, existing illustrations and relevant comments
   before editing. Compare *what the user liked* to *what the user disliked*.
   A disliked page's copy can be replaced while its successful composition
   survives. Source examples are evidence of intent, not automatic deployment
   rights or a reason to publish personal artwork.
2. Recover current work from [the Content Design corpus](../561-group-design-bible.md),
   original Group brand tokens and relevant operations, web or print producers.
   Recover upstream standards and actual consumer capabilities at their **named
   versions**. For this kind of work the comparators include the
   [DTCG 2025.10 Format Module](https://www.w3.org/community/reports/design-tokens/CG-FINAL-format-20251028/),
   [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), and the actual requirements of
   the target medium. DTCG is a W3C *Community Group* report, not a W3C
   Recommendation.
3. For matched industry competitors, separate jobs: **Style Dictionary**
   translates token sets to platform artifacts; **Storybook/Chromatic**
   supports component visual snapshots and reviewed baselines; **Playwright**
   exercises a real browser page. Do not claim the Group has met, much less
   beaten, an entire product by parsing a token file. Establish comparable
   inputs, output surfaces, time/energy/compute costs where measured, refusal
   behavior, independent practical acceptance and repeatability.
4. Translate evidence into separately checkable requirement statements:
   readable task, authentic voice, correct type hierarchy, token/geometry
   conformance, accessibility, source attribution, useful actions, lawful
   ownership, visual acceptance. A hypothesis or a preference should remain
   labeled as such.

**Research output:** a source-qualified set of requirements and falsifiers
linked to the existing Work. A mood board and a scientific paper are not
interchangeable. Research must not imply that a hypothesized philosophical
distinction is proved simply because it has a striking poster.

## Technology gesture — repair the original makers

5. Choose the existing producer: Group Content Design owns the visual language
   and DTCG tokens; Red Cup Web Theming translates reusable token/contrast
   contracts; TUFTE and the authorized site application compose real pages;
   Red Cup Visual Asset Generation composes separately licensed assets. Reuse
   the closest admitted template per [Template fork](template-fork.md).
   If source, package export, renderer, asset storage or operations reader is
   missing, repair *that seam*, not a new independent CSS copy.
6. Keep values in the source-owned token profile. Derive CSS, printable rules
   and other medium outputs from the same approved inputs. Preserve the house's
   three type channels, geometric grids, named color roles, restrained accents,
   numbered folio, semantic captions and original client context.
   Automatically generated images never themselves certify source, licensing
   or author approval.
7. Exercise positive and **adverse** conformance tests. A brand-critical
   change must fail if the colors mutate, the typography is replaced by system
   fonts, a numbered mark vanishes, the layout clips at a supported viewport,
   a page introduces an identity-led claim that its owner rejected, a figure
   loses provenance, or keyboard readers cannot see focus.
8. Make a *real rendered artifact* with the original engine, at least one
   desktop and one phone viewport when the output is web, and a relevant print
   specimen when print is an actual target. Retain dated screenshots or
   print proofs, source commit, fonts, named media modes, and configuration
   versions. A CSS string test is not proof of computed browser style.
   Screenshots without a separately approved golden reference are
   **candidate evidence**, not automatically accepted or rejected diffs.

For the Group use [Color-budget application](color-budget-application.md):
alarm-red bounding-box coverage at most 6%, witness-blue at most 3%.
Text color contrast and link discernibility are separate requirements.
Do **not** print red text on bone at body size merely because the mark uses
red; the Design Bible identifies that contrast failure.

## Practice gesture — independent receiving, then improve the producer

9. Deliver the exact edition, output, evidence and a legible review packet
   through the original Work/receiving lane. The reviewer should see the
   artifact *before* reading its internal issue. Ask whether (a) the intended
   job is clear, (b) the visual feels continuous with the approved reference,
   (c) the content makes no unsupported identity/performance claim,
   (d) focus/motion/zoom work, and (e) any expressive departure is intentional.
   An assayer must be separate from the builder when independent acceptance
   is required.
10. Record accepted, rejected, contested or *not yet independently observed*
    against the specific tested artifact and source revision. A merged PR,
    passing code test, successful Cloudflare deploy, or screenshot archive
    proves only its own bounded stage. Do not relabel it as buyer approval.
11. For an accepted variation, retain the exact applicable client/surface.
    Promote a truly reusable template only through the
    [three-independent-uses rule](template-fork.md). When a real counterexample
    exposes a flaw in this procedure, edit the original source and **use the
    repaired method on the next authorized Work**, measuring whether rework
    or the recipient's rejection actually decreases.

## Aesthetic and affective meet-then-beat — comparative public receiving (2026-10-23)

**Demand and owner.** This is a specialization of the **same** commissioning,
fabrication and independent-receiving procedure for the Group's original
[corporate public-legibility Work #259](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-services-section/issues/259)
and [561.group receiving Work #17](https://github.com/561-group/561.group/issues/17),
not an independent marketing roadmap, universal affect score or new brand law.
Science owns hypotheses and research validity; this Group source owns visual
decisions; Red Cup owns reusable production/instruments; Coesite coordinates
work and comparison; Causeway/public audience and competent editors receive
actual expressive work. A tested component is not an emotionally received work.

**Select *different* public comparators for different jobs.** The
[WARC Creative 100 (2026)](https://www.warc.com/en/data-benchmarks/rankings-creative-100-overview)
ranks awarded creativity (Heineken, Apple, McDonald's lead its brands).
[Cannes Lions 2026](https://www.canneslions.com/news/final-winners-announced-for-2026)
names Heineken, KitKat and IKEA as Creative Brands of the Year, and Apple as
an in-house agency leader. The
[WARC Effective 100 (2026)](https://www.warc.com/en/data-benchmarks/rankings-effective-100-overview)
separately ranks awarded effectiveness (McDonald's, Dove, IKEA lead brands);
the [Effie Index released June 2026](https://www.effieindex.com/ranking/?rt=6)
ranks Unilever, McDonald's and AB InBev as marketers.
[Kantar BrandZ 2026](https://www.kantar.com/campaigns/brandz/global) is a
brand-*value* ranking, not an aesthetic or affective acceptance test. Awarded,
effective, valuable and beloved are **four nonidentical claims**; none measures
this Group's own audience. Examples are *candidate craft comparators*, not
style templates or rights to copy their trade dress.

For each actual receiver/job, predeclare one same-job reference and an
original alternative: first-five-second corporate recognition; a technical
reader finding a real source and distinguishing proof from proposal; a buyer
finding the responsible service and contact; a viewer comprehending an original
character's motive and consequence; or a voluntary supporter understanding
what their support preserves. Compare **matched mediums and exposures**:
one screen against one screen, one complete film against one complete film,
one interaction against one interaction. Record the peer's original campaign
and dated version and the Group's source revision, channel, viewport, timing,
language, accessibility mode, and legal permission to use a specimen.
A large brand's distribution, paid impressions and budget are not quality
scores; inaccessible or unlicensed peer material is a refused comparison.

**Use the source-owned research without converting it into invented evidence.**
[Science's affect term](https://github.com/lenticule-science/561-theory/blob/main/science/theories/medicine/theories/psychology/terms/affect.md)
distinguishes bodily affect (including valence/arousal) from a person's named
emotion; self-report is a situated report, **not** a direct measure of an
unobserved nervous system.
The [state-aesthetic-continuum inquiry](https://github.com/lenticule-science/561-theory/blob/main/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/cybernetic-postliberalism/prose/teaching/explanations/the-state-aesthetic-continuum.md)
and [affect-governance analysis](https://github.com/lenticule-science/561-theory/blob/main/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/predicated-sociology/concepts/affect-governance.md)
warn against treating emotional capture as ethical success. In original
[Media Science #11](https://github.com/lenticule-science/media-services-section/issues/11),
apparent motive attributed to moving forms can diverge from source events;
rhythm and recognizable figures are *testable design hypotheses*, not verified
561 Group audience effects.

**Transfer causal creative practices, never borrowed visual signatures.**
These 2026-awarded examples give **specific, falsifiable production hypotheses**,
not evidence that their performance will transfer to this institution.

- [AXA *Three Words*](https://www.warc.com/en/campaigns/b94de73e3d4a4230811f1f12dcc2706b)
  changed the underlying insurance contract and emergency-relocation service.
  Hypothesis: an authenticated *institutional act and beneficiary consequence*
  is more persuasive than a picture promising help. Our film/page must follow
  a verifiable Work event, not invent an accomplished rescue or successful
  independent institution.
- [Spotify *Spreadbeats*](https://clios.com/winners-gallery/details/173291)
  performed a music video **inside the spreadsheet medium** used by advertising
  planners. Hypothesis: our actual inspectable mathematics, computing interfaces
  and source-linked research can be performed with medium-native surprise,
  rather than represented by stock glowing-brain or gradient images. Preserve
  an accessible alternative and the original proof/proposal boundary.
- [Heineken *For the Love of Pubs*](https://www.heineken.com/ie/en/campaigns/fortheloveofpubs)
  made a real community's effort to preserve a pub the subject of a story.
  Hypothesis: character, place, material repair and visible consequences carry
  affect better than claims of scale. When portraying our real communities or
  Indigenous practices, their own voices, dignity and rights have authority;
  not every lived relation is an exploitable story.
- [Dove *Real Beauty*](https://www.warc.com/en/data-benchmarks/rankings-effective-100-overview)
  demonstrates a long-running recognizable narrative platform rather than a
  new decorative brand each week. Hypothesis: **consistent but evolving**
  561 Group visual grammar and original characters across sites, publication,
  research, drama, service and small repeated interactions can accumulate
  recognition. The five Group voices remain distinct and Source/Public Media
  rights govern each act.

Our *candidate* expressive test is a **witnessed-consequence scene**:
an immediately legible object/person/character and situation; an unexpected
yet authentic rule, relation, action or refusal; a perceptible consequence;
and a source-linked opportunity for a viewer to learn or act. Visual craft
may use the authorized giant numeral, hard grid, restrained color and type,
source-owned mythopoetry, sequencing, sound, timing, negative space or genuine
participation appropriate to the **actual** medium and rights. Compare that
scene with a source-accurate conventional explanation of the **same** event:
does it improve unaided recognition, justified feeling, correct understanding
and voluntary next action without increasing false claims? An unmade scene
has no performance result. Animated characters and live works are commissioned
and edited by their original Public Media owners; a website test cannot certify
a film or an embodied Actor.

**Preregister an independently usable audience comparison for this job:**

1. Before showing production tickets or explaining the institution, observe
   unaided first-impression words and unprompted distinctive-mark recognition
   after a bounded exposure (record exposure seconds). Later ask for unaided
   recall of the actual work and its source, recording delay. Do not score
   participants by whether they agree with the Group's metaphysics.
2. Ask voluntary readers separately for perceived valence and arousal on
   anchored scales, free-word feeling description, interest/curiosity, perceived
   trustworthiness, and sense of agency. Distinguish stated feeling,
   interpretation and physiological claim. Record negative, mixed and
   ambivalent responses without recoding dissent as failure.
3. Observe the *real* task: destination reached, correct source/offer found,
   factual recall, action attempted, time, navigation/assistive-technology
   friction and voluntary follow-through where lawfully visible. Correct
   information and freely chosen refusal can both be good outcomes.
4. Have a separate qualified art/editorial recipient judge craft, visual
   hierarchy, scene coherence and originality against the adopted Group
   Design Bible and same-format peers; explain disagreement. Test accessibility,
   rights, culturally competent source authority and rejected identity-led
   persuasion as **independent hard constraints**, not weighted sentiment.
5. Name participant recruitment, consent, language/ability strata, sample
   denominators, display/order randomization or its absence, response
   noncoverage, data minimization and uncertainty **before** inspecting
   results. For a small pilot report observations as a pilot; do not present
   p-values or a universal “emotion” or “beat” score. Do not capture covert
   behavioral or biometric data.

The original [561.group Chromium visual witness](https://github.com/561-group/561.group/blob/main/scripts/assay-rendered-brand.mjs)
already produces exact source, viewport, screenshot hash and computed-style
observations. Extend **its existing REVIEW packet/CI artifact**, not a second
site or dashboard, to present real specimens and a consent-based offline
audience-reading worksheet. Actual respondent answers and source-qualified
peer observations must enter existing Work/receiving through the competent
office; a prepared worksheet, browser pixel mask, green build, view count or
favorable model judgment **never** certifies felt effects or independent
acceptance. Do not reuse an image in another venue merely because it appears
in an award gallery.

**Comparative decision and recurrence.** Record each measure, denominator,
counterexample and judgment separately; name exactly which peer/job reached
parity and which exceeded it, with uncertainty and an independently observed
beneficiary. A creative work can earn an aesthetic success without a sale;
a useful page can earn practical success without admiration. If no human
responses exist, the correct result is **not yet evaluated**. On rejected
artifacts improve the *actual source maker* (narrative, composition, typography,
pacing, figure, interaction or original producer), fabricate and test another
edition, then send it back to an independent audience. Neither broad
brand-value comparisons nor exposure-maximizing manipulation can close this
Work by proxy.

## Source-to-public-action receiving: witnessed-consequence first (2026-10-23)

An observed second test case now qualifies an important **medium-native choice**
inside the same original Group creative production job. The
[561.group consolidated source PR #49](https://github.com/561-group/561.group/pull/49)
retains the current public work/contact/payment-inquiry paths and restores
the four-house original field score alongside one specific *original service
event*: the original Causeway Green Web pilot page once returned HTTP 404,
then the original publisher's
[source and release repair](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-web-services-section/pull/27)
was independently observed at HTTP 200. The web medium can **show the real
404→200 consequence and offer the actual original
[free worksheet](https://services.561.group/green-web-service-pilot/)**,
rather than simply advertise a brand's ability to fix websites. This
transfers AXA's *work changes the underlying service* and Spotify's *medium
performs the point* mechanisms without copying either company's imagery
or claiming equal creative reception.

**Actual adverse receiving and source repair.** The original four-house
visual score was technically correct, but a real 390px full-page screenshot
showed the illustrative four-card narrative appearing *before* the documented
service event and usable visitor action. This placed the most concrete
answer late in the scrolling task, despite source tests and pixels passing.
Within the **same original** TUFTE renderer and Design Bible the composer
moved the documentarily witnessed consequence *ahead* of the explanatory
four-company graphic score. The existing Chromium runner now observes
`proofTop < cardsTop` as rendered DOM geometry at 390px and 1440px,
and rejects a future regression or a counterfeited off-domain action; the
existing source/HTML/JSON tests preserve the Group's recent real paid
service opportunities and qualified customer inquiry routes. This is a
specific practical layout/accessibility failure and corrected producer
selection, not a universal claim that one story order improves feelings.

**Qualify the full Work at each boundary:**

- Source-qualified original Group identity and actual Causeway publisher
  receipt: candidate documentary truth, not a live HTTP-uptime guarantee,
  external client satisfaction, ecological efficacy or independent service
  adoption. The effect has a dated source and human-inspectable original
  working destination.
- Installed TUFTE/Worker/Chromium result and local consent-first worksheet:
  syntax/native/browser *technical receiving only*. The browser assays
  one action's position, URL, 404→200 depiction, receipt links, source
  qualifications and four original visual cards, not actual traffic,
  accessibility across assistive technologies, intention or pleasantness.
- Independent art and actual site reader receiving: still unperformed.
  Different legitimate readers may prefer an abstract institutional story
  before a practical service, or decline the whole sequence. Compare the
  unchanged audience task, source-accurate plain alternative and actual
  expressive alternative under the same device/conditions, with readers'
  free choice and task success separate from stated valence/arousal.
- Economic receiving: a buyer's service use, sent quote, agreed scope,
  customer-accepted result and settled cash are separate native conditions;
  the source-of-truth public price and terms remain with Causeway, not a
  pretend checkout added to the Group's aesthetic experiment.

**Reusable procedural decision, limited to this same job:** where the
visitor is there to *find/understand/verify/use* a real service, present its
best source-qualified practical consequence and the reversible, optional
next action **before** an explanatory visual allegory, unless actual
independent reader evidence supports a different order. In creative media
whose audience job is fiction rather than service choice, this order is
*not* mandatory; return to scene/character consequence and independent
dramaturgical receiving. Refuse fake service receipts, fabricated emotion,
invented provider authority, a privacy-intrusive engagement score or
a victory claim derived from a passing source/test graph.
