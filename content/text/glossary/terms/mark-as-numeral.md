# Mark as numeral

The 561 mark is the numeral *561* set in Space Grotesk 700 with a single 8-px bracket rule above it.
There is no seal, no monogram, no figurative emblem, no tagline beneath.
The wordmark "GROUP" is a permitted lockup partner, not part of the mark.

A logo that is just a number is the bible's deliberate refusal of two adjacencies it does not want — corporate identity (which trades in seals and abstract emblems) and movement iconography (which trades in fists, suns, and fields).
The numeral is recognizable at any scale, reproducible by anyone with cardboard and a knife, survives photocopy and screen-print, and franchises poorly: a stencil of *561* cannot be stretched into a brand.

The mark's discipline (geometry, clearspace, color, minimum size, contracted variant) is specified in [five-six-one-mark](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/five-six-one-mark.md).
The four lockups (vertical, inline, foot, stamp) are specified in [group-wordmark](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/group-wordmark.md), [stamp-mark](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/stamp-mark.md), and [contracted-mark](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/contracted-mark.md).
The mark's photocopy survival is asserted as the [photocopy-survival theorem](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09).

Mark-as-numeral realizes [memetic repeatability](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/memetic-repeatability.md) (a digit string is the most repeatable mark a Latin reader can carry) and [felt weight](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/felt-weight.md) (the numeral block is the load-bearing visual mass on every artifact's masthead).

The mark also serves as a cipher: 561 is the area code of the part of the world the confederacy works in, but the bible refuses to spell that out repeatedly. § 00 names it once; once is plenty.
The cipher is the joke; jokes don't need a footnote on every page.
