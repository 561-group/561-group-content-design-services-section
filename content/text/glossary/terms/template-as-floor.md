# Template as floor

The five [examples](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s08) the bible ships in § 08 — slogan poster, assembly call, numeral banner, field-instruction pamphlet, web template — are *the floor, not the ceiling*.
Each is a structure, not a finished piece.
Copy is left as `[placeholder]`; fill before printing or publishing.

Three commitments follow.

1. **Copy a template before composing from scratch.** A template is the bible's accumulated answer to "what does this artifact format look like when 561 makes it?"
  Starting from the template means inheriting the [grid](grid-system.md), the [type-scale](type-scale.md) tokens, the [color-budget](color-budget.md) defaults, the caption format, and the colophon stamp without re-deciding any of them.
2. **The editor of an artifact decides whether to follow this template, fork it, or write a new one.** Templates are not hard requirements.
  An artifact whose form has no matching template is a permitted artifact — it just does not yet have a template.
  The editor's responsibility is to make the artifact, not to fit it into a template that does not serve it.
3. **New templates earn a § 08.
  N entry only after the council has approved a third use.** A one-off variant is not a template.
  A template is a form the bible has watched survive three independent uses.
  The [template-fork morphism](../../documents/articles/specifications/procedures/template-fork.md) gives the procedure.

The discipline is what keeps the bible's template inventory honest.
Without it, every artifact would tend to spawn a new template entry, and the bible would calcify into a catalog.
With it, the template inventory grows only when the work has demonstrated that a form is durable.

Template-as-floor realizes [memetic repeatability](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/memetic-repeatability.md): a template that has survived three independent uses is, by that fact, a form that the work has demonstrated is reproducible.
