# Voice as specificity

Voice in the bible is not "tone of voice" in the brand-guide sense.
It is the operative discipline that the [three rules](the-three-rules.md) name: *be specific, use verbs, refuse the philanthropic register*.

Three working rules carry the weight.

1. **Sentences are short because readers are tired.** A two-clause sentence with a verb in the middle reads at a glance; a three-clause sentence with two qualifying phrases reads as a paragraph.
  The slab body of the bible is set at 50–70 characters per line because more characters per line make even a short sentence look like a paragraph.
2. **Numbers are exact because vague numbers read as marketing.** "Three acres" reads as a fact.
  "A few acres" reads as a hedge.
  "Our region" reads as a marketing line a development office wrote.
  If you do not have an exact number yet, leave the slot empty rather than fill it with mood.
3. **Names are real because anonymous "kin" reads like a stock photo.** When a person, a watershed, a town, a date can be named, name it.
  Anonymizing for safety is a real reason; anonymizing for warmth is the donor-appeal register and is forbidden.

The voice rules are operationalized at proof time by the [voice-substitution morphism](../../documents/articles/specifications/procedures/voice-substitution.md), which gives the canonical don't / instead pairs ("empowering communities" → "name who, doing what"; "stakeholders" → "people, by name when you can"; "we are committed to" → "we did, we will, we won't"; "solutions, ecosystems, journeys" → "use the actual word").

Voice realizes [orientation before interpretation](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/orientation-before-interpretation.md): the reader is told *what specifically is being claimed, by whom, about what* before any interpretive language begins.
The philanthropic register collapses orientation into mood; voice-as-specificity refuses the collapse.
