# Refusal as design method

The bible's primary load-bearing operator is *refusal*. § 09 — the longest section — is a list of seven visual moves the bible explicitly does not make, each carrying a check the artifact can fail before it prints.
The other sections are saturated with smaller refusals: the mark refuses metallic finishes, italic, drop shadow, and subtle gray; the slogan refuses centered set, mixed case, and any rotation other than 0°; the type stack refuses humanist serifs and weight-mixing within a family on a single line; the color system refuses gradients, P3, and "subtle" tints.

A refusal is not a stylistic preference.
It is an operator the editor of an artifact applies before the proof goes to print: *if the artifact would do X, reject the artifact*.
Refusals are stated with their checks because the bible's discipline is that any rule the artifact cannot be tested against at proof time does not belong in the bible — it belongs in the [operating documents](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s00) of 561 itself.

The reason refusal is the primary operator (and not, e.g., positive prescription) is that the bible's design environment is one of constant aesthetic drift toward two attractors: the donor-appeal register (warm, soft-focus, mission-statement-coded) and the agritech-pitch register (thin geometric sans, green gradient, drone footage).
Without active refusal, both attractors win every proof cycle.
The refusal list is shaped by what the work has actually drifted toward; new refusals are added when the bible has seen a drift fail twice.

The seven refusals of § 09 are decomposed as theorems in this corpus: [no-philanthropic-visual-language](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09), [no-agritech-minimalism](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09), [no-type-set-over-photography](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09), [no-ai-generated-imagery](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09), [no-co-marks-beside-the-numeral](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09), [no-metallic-foil-emboss](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09), [no-third-party-requests-on-web-surfaces](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09).
