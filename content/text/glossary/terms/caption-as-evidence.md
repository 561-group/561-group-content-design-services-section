# Caption as evidence

Every figure in a 561 artifact carries the same five-field caption.

```
FIG ##.# — [what]. [who]. [where]. [when]. [source / rights].
```

Periods between fields.
Empty fields drop.
Set in [Roboto Slab 11/14](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/type-stack.md) at color `--ink-soft` (token M1 of the [type-scale](type-scale.md)).
`FIG ##.#` set in the same family at the same size, weight 700.
Left-aligned to the figure's left edge; max width equals the figure's width.

The caption format is uniform because uniformity makes the caption *parsable at a glance* — a reader skimming a long-form artifact can scan the captions in any column and reconstruct the artifact's evidentiary spine without reading the body.
The five fields are also the bare minimum to satisfy the bible's voice-as-specificity rule: every figure announces *what it is, who is in it or made it, where it was taken or made, when, and where the source / rights live*.
A figure missing more than one of the five is a defect at proofing.

The caption morphism — [caption-setting](../../documents/articles/specifications/procedures/caption-setting.md) — gives the geometry, color, and edge-alignment rules.
The caption is also the entry point for [witness-blue](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/witness-blue.md): when a caption points to a quantity or a date that is the artifact's evidentiary claim, that quantity or date may be set in witness-blue.
The default ink-soft is the rest of the caption.

Caption-as-evidence realizes [orientation before interpretation](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/orientation-before-interpretation.md) at the figure level: the reader is oriented to *what they are looking at* before any interpretive reading begins.
