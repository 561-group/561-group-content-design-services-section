# Hazard / evidence pair

The bible's two functional colors carry one job each.

**Red flags hazard or urgency.** Use [alarm-red](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/alarm-red.md) for: deadlines, refusals (the strikethrough in § 06), the single word in the [slogan](slogan-as-argument.md) that is currently most at stake on a given artifact.
Do not use it for emphasis, for headers, for *look here*, for ornament, or for more than one word in any block of text.

**Blue flags evidence.** Use [witness-blue](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/witness-blue.md) for: figure callouts (`FIG 03.x`), data quantities, source links, dates of record.
Do not use it as a body link color, as a fill, or anywhere a sentence is not pointing at a fact.

The two colors are a *pair* and not a palette: each is defined against the other's job.
Red is the color of *what you must look at right now*.
Blue is the color of *here is the source for what you just read*.
A reader of a 561 artifact who sees both colors learns within one or two artifacts what each one means and from then on can scan an artifact's red to find the urgency and an artifact's blue to find the evidence — without reading the body.

The pair realizes [glance direction](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/glance-direction.md): the eye is told where to land on first pass (red), where to verify on second pass (blue), and where to read at length (ink on bone).
The pair also realizes [peripheral uptake](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/peripheral-uptake.md): the colors are saturated and rare enough to register at the edge of vision before a focused read begins.

The pair is enforced by the [color-budget](color-budget.md) discipline.
Misuse of the colors (red as ornament, blue as a fill) is one of the bible's most common drift modes; the [color-budget-application morphism](../../documents/articles/specifications/procedures/color-budget-application.md) is the proof-time check that catches it.
