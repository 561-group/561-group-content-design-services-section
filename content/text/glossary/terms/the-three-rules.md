# The three rules

Three rules carry most of the weight of the bible.

1. **Be specific.** Three acres beats "our region."
  A name beats "kin."
  A date beats "soon."
  If you do not have a real number or a real name yet, leave the slot empty rather than filling it with mood.
2. **Use verbs.** "We grow," not "we are growers."
  "We did.
  We will.
  We won't." not "we are committed to."
  The slab body of the bible is set in [Roboto Slab](../../documents/articles/specifications/elements/type-stack.md) at 16/24 because verbs land best when each sentence has room to land.
3. **Refuse the philanthropic register.** No *impact*, no *mission*, no *stakeholders*, no *community*.
  The donor-appeal vocabulary of the development office reads as a stock photo regardless of who writes it.

The three rules are the bible's most-cited concept.
[Voice as specificity](voice-as-specificity.md) is the morphism-level expansion; [no philanthropic visual language](../../documents/articles/specifications/561-group-design-bible-v0.3.html#s09) is the visual refusal that makes the third rule visible at proof time.
The [voice-substitution morphism](../../documents/articles/specifications/procedures/voice-substitution.md) gives the operative don't / instead pairs.

The three rules realize the parent visual-engineering term [orientation before interpretation](../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/services/visual-engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/orientation-before-interpretation.md): the artifact must orient the reader to *what is being claimed*, by whom, about what, before any interpretation begins.
A nonprofit register collapses orientation into mood, which is exactly what the three rules refuse.
