FOLIO 002
§ 00 · PREAMBLE

This bible is not a brand. It is a tool. A brand sells. A tool works. We are building a visual language for a confederacy that intends to feed itself, govern itself, and remember itself — without apology to the institutions that engineered our hunger.

The 561 Group is an autonomous confederacy organized to pursue black liberation, indigenous sovereignty, and the abolition of food apartheid. 561 = E·F·A = End Food Apartheid. The number is the first instruction. Everything that follows is in service of it.

This document is a working manual. It does not require taste; it requires obedience to the constraints, and disobedience to the institutions those constraints stand against. When the manual fails, the manual is wrong. Edit it.

FOLIO 003
§ 01
MISSION
A Confederacy
Against Hunger.

We organize land, seed, water, labor, and memory toward the end of food apartheid — the engineered scarcity that has shaped Black and Indigenous life in this hemisphere for five centuries. This is not a campaign. It is a confederacy.

WHAT WE ARE

An autonomous network of growers, cooks, archivists, organizers, designers, and kin — bound by mutual aid agreements, not by ownership.

WHAT WE ARE NOT

A nonprofit. A brand. A startup. A subcontractor of state agriculture. A cultural product to be consumed.

WHAT WE BUILD

Land trusts. Seed libraries. Field kitchens. Distribution corridors. Printing presses. Schools. Records. Each one a fact on the ground.

WHAT WE END

The food apartheid system: the redlines, the deserts, the surplus-cheese economy, the supply-chain capture of Black and Indigenous land.

FIVE OBLIGATIONS
OF THE WORK
  1. 01. Feed first. Theorize second.
  2. 02. Land returns. Seed returns. Story returns.
  3. 03. Refuse the philanthropic frame.
  4. 04. Document everything. Archive nothing extractable.
  5. 05. Make work that survives without electricity.
PRIMARY DECLARATION ▸
No Land. No Liberation.
No Seed. No Sovereignty.

FOLIO 005
§ 02
NAME · SYMBOLISM
561 Is The
First Instruction.
561
5 → E · 6 → F · 1 → A
A1Z26 simple substitution.

561 = EFA = End Food Apartheid. The number is a key, not a symbol. Read it letter-by-letter and the program reveals itself. The numerals are spoken plainly — five-six-one — never “five-sixty-one.” The substitution is the thing.

WHY A NUMBER

A number is harder to brand. It does not perform care. It does not say its meaning at a glance. It asks a question, and the answer is the work.

WHY THE WORD “APARTHEID”

Because “food desert” is geological — it makes hunger sound like weather. Apartheid is a policy. We name the policy.

Construction
& Use.

The seal is not a logo. It is a certification: when affixed to a thing — a publication, a kitchen, a corridor — it asserts that the thing was made in accordance with the obligations of §01.

FOLIO 012
§ 04
COLOR
Two Inks.
One Surface.

A risograph palette enforces discipline: two spot inks on bone paper, plus ink-black for type and rule. Misregistration is not a defect. It is the visible evidence that the work was made by hand on a real machine.

FIELD NOTE — Black is reserved for ink and rule. Bone is reserved for paper. Red and Blue are the only colors that may carry meaning. A fifth color introduced by a designer is a fifth ration on a four-ration table.
