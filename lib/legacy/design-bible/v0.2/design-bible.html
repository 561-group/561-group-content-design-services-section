§ 00 · A NOTE ON THIS DOCUMENT

This is scaffolding, not doctrine. The bible holds the visual constraints — color, type, grid, mark — and leaves the political voice to those who carry the work. Where you see a [placeholder], the council fills it. Where you see a refusal, it is binding.

FOLIO 003
§ 01
MISSION
The Slogan.
Land.
Water.
Language.
Culture.

Four words. Read together, they name the program. Land is held in trust. Water is shared, not sold. Language is spoken, taught, and refused-on-our-own-terms. Culture is what survives the first three.

USAGE
  • — Always set in this order. The order is the argument.
  • — Period after each word. They are not a list; they are four sentences.
  • — One word may be set in red. Never two.
  • — May be stacked, may be inline. Never centered, never italicized.
  • — Do not abbreviate. Do not acronymize. Do not translate without consent.

§ 02
THE MARK
561.

The mark is the numeral, set in Space Grotesk 700, with a single 8-px rule above it. That is the entire mark. No ring, no tagline, no co-brand. Its meaning is held by kin; the mark does not explain itself.

Refused Circular seals. Drop shadows. Embossing. Foil. 3D. Outlines. Italics on the numeral. Stacked numeral & letterform pairings (e.g. 561/EFA). The mark stands alone or it does not stand.

§ 03
COLOR
Two Inks. One Surface.

Bone paper, ink, one alarm, one witness. Red is alarm — a thing you should not look past. Blue is witness — a thing you should slow down for. They are not interchangeable, and they are never decorative.

Misregistration on print is permitted and welcome. It is the visible evidence that a person, not a brand, made the thing.

§ 04
TYPE
Sans & Slab.

Space Grotesk for structure: marks, headlines, labels. Roboto Slab for body and the slogan. JetBrains Mono for metadata: folios, captions, dates, filenames.

A constructivist pairing. The body face is the slab so that the body of the work reads as weight, not as flow. Sentences are short. The reader is busy.

§ 05
GRID
Twelve Columns.

A 12-column grid with 24 px gutters. The structure is visible in development and load-bearing in production. Orientation precedes interpretation: every page begins with a folio, a section number, and a section title at the same coordinates.

Spacing scale follows: 4 · 8 · 14 · 24 · 48 · 80 · 120. Rule weights: 1 · 3 · 8. Anything else is improvisation, and improvisation must be defended.

§ 07
IMAGERY
Woodcut. Photo as Evidence.

Two registers. Woodcut figuration for the human, commissioned from member-artists. Photography for the evidentiary, treated as duotone or line-screen, captioned with what · who · where · when · source.

No AI imagery. No stock. No type-on-photo overlays. Photographs are evidence; evidence is captioned, not designed-on.

FOLIO 024
§ 09
REFUSALS
What We Don't Do.

A design system is also a refusal. The list is binding.

When in doubt, refuse. The strongest move is the move not to make a thing.

  • No philanthropic visual language — soft focus, smiling beneficiaries, white-text-over-photo.
  • No agritech minimalism — sans on green gradients, drone footage of fields.
  • No appropriation of Indigenous textile, beadwork, or regalia patterns. Honor through structure, not surface.
  • No type set over photography. Photography is evidence, captioned only.
  • No AI-generated imagery, ever, full stop.
  • No co-marks beside the 561 numeral. The mark stands alone.
  • No seal in gold, silver, foil, embossing, or 3D.
  • No trackers, pixels, analytics, or ads on any 561 surface.
  • No design work for corporations whose business sustains food apartheid, settler land tenure, or carceral logistics.
  • No use of community, stakeholder, beneficiary, impact, brand, or mission.
FOLIO 026
§ 10
LINEAGE
Two Practices. Cited.

This bible follows the framework of visual engineering as articulated by emsenn — design oriented toward orientation-before-interpretation and the refusal of premature closure. Two design lineages converge in the work.

LEAD · BRUTALISM

Anti-ornament, hard hierarchy, exposed structure. Visible in the masthead, the section heads, the rule weights, the refusal of decoration. The page tells you it is a page.

LEAD · TUFTE

Evidence-first, attention budget honored, micro/macro reading. Visible in the caption convention, the table of color-budget rules, and the forensic register applied to records.

VISUAL ANCESTRIES — STOOD WITH, NOT TAKEN FROM

Constructivist agitprop — El Lissitzky, Rodchenko, Stepanova. Diagonal hierarchy, type-as-construction, photomontage as evidence. We borrow the discipline; we do not borrow the iconography.

Indigenous textile logic — strip-construction, additive panels, repaired-in-place. Honored by structural analogy in our grid and modular templates. Never by motif extraction.

COLOPHON
Edits Welcome.

v.0.2 — drafted May 2026. The council reviews on the equinoxes. Edits enter via the corridor councils, not by fiat.

If you want to make something for the 561 Group, follow the bible. Where the bible fails you, propose an edit. Where the bible contradicts the work, the work wins.

Reproduction by members is permitted. Resale is forbidden. Translation is encouraged; simplification is not.
