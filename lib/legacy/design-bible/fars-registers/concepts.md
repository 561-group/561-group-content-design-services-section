# Concepts

The recurring disciplines the bible names.
A concept is a name for a discipline that crosses sections — a thing the bible expects every contributor to recognize and apply by default.
Concepts have no `status` field; they are the bible's vocabulary and persist across editions.

Each concept realizes one or more parent [visual-engineering terms](../../../terms/) and is depended on by objects, morphisms, theorems, and examples downstream.

## Inventory

- [the-three-rules](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/the-three-rules.md) — be specific, use verbs, refuse the philanthropic register.
- [refusal-as-design-method](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/refusal-as-design-method.md) — refusal is the bible's primary load-bearing operator.
- [bible-as-living-document](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/bible-as-living-document.md) — the bible is a working manual, not a finished system.
- [slogan-as-argument](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/slogan-as-argument.md) — Land · Water · Language · Culture is a sequence of dependencies, not a list.
- [mark-as-numeral](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/mark-as-numeral.md) — a logo that is just a number resists franchising and stencils with cardboard.
- [color-budget](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/color-budget.md) — color is rationed by inked-area percentage, not styled.
- [hazard-evidence-pair](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/hazard-evidence-pair.md) — red flags hazard or urgency; blue flags evidence.
- [type-scale](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/type-scale.md) — three families, nine size tokens, one job each.

- [grid-system](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/grid-system.md) — twelve columns on screen, fourteen on print; baseline-locked.
- [baseline-grid](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/baseline-grid.md) — text-bearing matter snaps to the document baseline; photographs do not.
- [voice-as-specificity](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/voice-as-specificity.md) — three acres beats "our region"; verbs over nouns; refuse the philanthropic register.
- [two-registers](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/two-registers.md) — commissioned artwork for the human register; photography, treated as evidence, for the documentary register.
- [caption-as-evidence](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/caption-as-evidence.md) — every figure carries the same five fields, periods between, empty fields drop.
- [template-as-floor](template-as-floor.md) — templates are the floor, not the ceiling; fork or write a new one and earn the entry.
- [no-third-party-on-web](../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/no-third-party-on-web.md) — one origin, no cookies, no third-party requests on any 561 web surface.
