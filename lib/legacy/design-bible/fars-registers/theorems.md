# Theorems

In this bible, a theorem is a falsifiable claim about an artifact: a refusal carrying a check the artifact can fail before it prints. § 09 of the canonical source is structured this way (refusal + check), and the bible's other clauses marked "Test" or "Check" follow the same shape.
Each theorem here states the refusal, the check that decides it, and the morphisms or objects it constrains.

Each theorem's frontmatter declares its `class: refusal | test | conflict-rule`, status, and the objects, concepts, and morphisms it `depends-on` and `see-also`.
