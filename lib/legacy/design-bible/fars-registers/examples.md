# Examples

The five starting templates the bible ships in § 08.
Each is a structure, not a finished piece.
Copy is left as `[placeholder]`; fill before printing or publishing.
Each template specifies trim, bleed, fold, paper, ink, and source-file location, plus the test the printed artifact must pass.

Templates are the floor, not the ceiling.
The editor of an artifact decides whether to follow this template, fork it, or write a new one.
New templates earn a § 08.
N entry only after the council has approved a third use; see [template-fork](../../../../content/text/documents/articles/specifications/procedures/template-fork.md).

Each example's frontmatter declares `instantiates:` (the morphisms it composes) and `depends-on:` (the objects it uses).

## Inventory

- [slogan-poster](slogan-poster.md) — § 08.1.
  18 × 24 in, two-color (ink + alarm).
  Declarative public-address artifact for wheatpaste, gallery, demonstration, window.
- [assembly-call](assembly-call.md) — § 08.2.
  11 × 17 in, ink ground with reverse type and alarm date.
  For meetings, hearings, gatherings, and assemblies that 561 calls.
- [numeral-banner](numeral-banner.md) — § 08.3.
  18 × 24 in poster or 36 × 48 in banner, one-color (ink).
  For wheatpaste runs, march banners, and presence-claims where the numeral itself is the message.
- [field-instruction-pamphlet](field-instruction-pamphlet.md) — § 08.4.
  8.5 × 11 in tri-fold, two-color (ink + alarm step numerals).
  For instruction-bearing artifacts.
- [web-static-site](web-static-site.md) — § 08.5.
  Static HTML, no framework, no build step, twelve-column grid; total page weight < 200 KB; functions without JavaScript.
