# Questions

Open questions the bible's editor recognizes but has not yet ruled on.
A question becomes a theorem (a refusal with a check) or a morphism (a procedure with a test) once the council has seen it fail twice and writes the rule.
Until then, it stays here so contributors know what is unsettled.

Each question carries a `status: open | provisional | deferred`, a short prompt, the entities it touches, and the kind of evidence that would close it.
